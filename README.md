# RETINUE

A small necromancer autobattler in the browser. You are the **Monarch**, a necromancer who stands in its
own camp, never strikes, and leads an army of the dead down four floors, choosing the route room by room.
The dead keep the plan: before each battle you place your souls, stack them, and draw the line each one will
march; then the battle plays out on its own, your army doing exactly what you drew while the living enemy
comes down its roads and improvises. Foes slain within your domain rise to fight for you. Slay the Hollow
Sovereign at the bottom of floor 4 to clear the run, then descend into the endless deep for as long as you
last. If the Monarch falls, the run is over.

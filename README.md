# RETINUE

A small necromancer autobattler in the browser. You are the **Monarch**, a necromancer who stands in its
own camp, never strikes, and leads an army of the dead down four floors, choosing the route room by room.
The dead hold the line: before each battle you upgrade your kinds, fuse souls into colossi, and place and
stack your pieces around the Monarch's seat; then the battle plays out on its own, every piece fighting from
the cell you gave it while the living enemy comes down its roads (or over the walls) and improvises. Foes
slain within your domain rise to fight for you. Slay the Hollow Sovereign at the bottom of floor 4 to clear
the run, then descend into the endless deep for as long as you last. If the Monarch falls, the run is over.
